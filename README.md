# Elecora — Electricity Market & Infrastructure Investment Analytics Platform

## ⚡ Overview

**Elecora** is a full-stack **Electricity Market & Infrastructure Investment Analytics Platform** that combines concepts from **Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Energy Economics, Financial Modeling, Investment Analysis, and Strategic Decision-Making**.

The platform is designed to evaluate power infrastructure projects such as **solar, wind, and hybrid energy projects** by analyzing electricity tariffs, generation capacity, utilization, CAPEX, OPEX, revenue, cash flow, NPV, IRR, and investment payback.

Elecora provides a structured environment for comparing different investment assumptions and understanding the financial impact of power-sector decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

Elecora is designed to help users:

- Analyze electricity infrastructure projects
- Evaluate power generation economics
- Track electricity market prices
- Estimate annual energy generation
- Calculate project revenue
- Analyze operating expenses
- Evaluate capital expenditure
- Calculate Net Present Value (NPV)
- Calculate Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
- Estimate investment payback period
- Compare investment scenarios
- Analyze tariff and utilization sensitivity
- Support strategic energy investment decisions

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Power Project Portfolio Management

Elecora allows users to create and manage power infrastructure projects.

Each project contains:

- Project name
- Technology type
- Installed capacity
- CAPEX
- OPEX
- Electricity tariff
- Capacity utilization
- Project lifetime

Supported project types can include:

- Solar
- Wind
- Solar + Battery
- Hybrid Renewable Energy
- Other power infrastructure

---

- Installed capacity
